title=Prepackaged (testing)
description=[h1]PREPACKAGED[/h1]
description=[b]A mod pack, set up for you.[/b]
description=
description=[b]In testing:[/b] expect bugs, and the pack format may still change between updates. Please report problems on [url=https://github.com/zexyqag/prepackaged/issues]GitHub[/url].
description=
description=Prepackaged turns a mod pack into a guided setup. A pack is a small Workshop item with one text file that lists the pack's mods. Prepackaged finds it and enables those mods in the pack's load order, after showing you exactly what changes.
description=
description=[h2]USE A PACK[/h2]
description=Subscribe to Prepackaged and the pack, then press Prepackaged in the bottom right corner of the main menu. Pick the pack, tick the optional mods you want and check the changes. Your old mod list is saved as a Mods screen preset first.
description=
description=[h2]KEEP YOUR OWN MODS[/h2]
description=Add your own mods, such as maps, on top of the pack. When the pack updates, only the pack's own mods change; yours stay. Save the result as your own preset with one click.
description=
description=[h2]MAKE A PACK[/h2]
description=A pack is a mod that holds a prepackaged.txt file. The format is described on [url=https://github.com/zexyqag/prepackaged]GitHub[/url].
description=
description=[h2]GOOD TO KNOW[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Packs can also be dropped in Zomboid/Lua/Prepackaged/packs or pasted from the clipboard.
description=[*]Nothing changes until you press Apply.
description=[*]Planned: key bind and mod option defaults, sandbox settings, server setup and a subscription helper.
description=[/list]
