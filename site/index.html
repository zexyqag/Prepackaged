Prepackaged map packs

Build a pack of Build 42 map mods from the Zomboid Map Index: add maps from the list, or let Suggest fill the pack for what you care about. Prepackaged sets it up in the game.

Your pack

      Click a cell to see what is on it.

      The list is the pack's map order (Map=): where two of its maps overlap, the one higher up wins those cells.

      Suggest

      Fills the pack with the maps that do best by your priorities, without two of them on one cell.

      Priorities

        Each counts as its share of the most any map in the index has, for or against, times its weight: at 10 for more cells, the biggest map scores 10.

        Limits

        Fill keeps the maps in your pack, overlaps and all, and adds to them; a map you take out stays out of suggestions until you add it again.

        Maps

        Map modWorkshop itemCellsSubscribersKeptRatingScoreNotes

        Kept: the subscribers it has of all it ever had. Rating: Steam's score from the up and down votes, which trusts more votes more. Score: what the map and what it needs bring to a pack by your weights.

        Pack file

        Letters, digits, _ and -, up to 48.

        In the game, subscribe to the pack's Workshop items, press Prepackaged in the main menu and Paste a pack, or put the downloaded file in Zomboid/Lua/Prepackaged/packs. For a dedicated server, the pack's Server button in Prepackaged gives the server's lines.
